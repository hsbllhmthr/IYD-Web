Indonesian Youth Diplomacy

Indonesian Youth Diplomacy (IYD) is an Indonesia-based non-profit and non-political youth organization that seeks to promote international exposure for Indonesian youths through inclusive empowerment. Established in 2013, IYD was originally a platform that connected alumni of Y20 delegates from Indonesia.

Who we are

Indonesian Youth Diplomacy (IYD) is a non-profit youth organization aiming to promote international exposure and drive inclusive empowerment for Indonesian youths to be influential voices at the national and international levels.

About IYD IYD Board Programs

IYD has been growing since 2013 from jakarta to 13 provinces across indonesia with a total more than 2000 youths engagement

IYD alumni give back to the country through various work that they do as government officials, diplomats, international public servants, members of parliament, start-up founders, investors at venture capital firms, management consultants, researchers, and academics.
