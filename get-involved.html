Get Involved

IYD has been growing since 2013
from Jakarta to 13 provinces across Indonesia
with a total more than 2000 youths engagement.

Indonesian Youth Diplomacy (IYD) has expanded from Jakarta to various regions across Indonesia, with a primary focus on establishing Local Chapters (LC) in several provinces to empower youth in international diplomacy. Based on the update as of August 2023, IYD introduced 8 new Local Chapters, extending its reach to the following provinces:

Indonesia Youth Diplomacy DKI Jakarta (Pusat)
Indonesia Youth Diplomacy LC Sulawesi Selatan
Indonesia Youth Diplomacy LC Sulawesi Utara
Indonesia Youth Diplomacy LC Sumatera Barat
Indonesia Youth Diplomacy LC Sumatera Utara
Indonesia Youth Diplomacy LC Kalimantan Selatan
Indonesia Youth Diplomacy LC Jawa Timur
Indonesia Youth Diplomacy LC Bali
Indonesia Youth Diplomacy LC Maluku
Indonesia Youth Diplomacy LC Kalimantan Timur
